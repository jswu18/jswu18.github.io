I am currently a machine learning engineer at <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/">Bloomberg</a>. Previously I was building high-dimensional probabilistic models for US electricity markets at a trading firm, <a href="https://www.gridmatic.com">Gridmatic</a>. Before that, I was at <a href="https://www.revolut.com">Revolut</a> working on time-series forecasting and NLP.

I completed my MSc. in <a href="https://ucl-ellis.github.io">Computational Statistics and Machine Learning (CSML)</a> at <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk">University College London</a>, working on generalised variational inference for Gaussian processes with <a href="https://jeremiasknoblauch.github.io">Jeremias Knoblauch</a> in the Department of Statistical Science. I also hold a BASc. in <a href="https://engsci.utoronto.ca/">Engineering Science</a> at the <a href="https://www.utoronto.ca/">University of Toronto</a> with a specialisation in robotics. 

My research interests lie in the areas of uncertainty quantification and variational inference.
